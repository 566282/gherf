# Privacy Policy

Operational draft based on implemented platform behavior as of 2026-08-05. This draft should be reviewed by legal counsel before production publication.

## 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how the Go4Wealth platform collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects personal information when you browse public pages, create an account, sign in, complete tasks, participate in rewards and promotions, use wallet and withdrawal features, interact with campaigns, or communicate with the platform.

## 2. Information we collect

### Account and profile information

The implemented service may collect:

- Full name.
- Email address.
- Password credentials handled through authentication services.
- Chosen account role.
- Referral code and referred-by code.
- Avatar URL and profile details you choose to add.
- Membership level, badges, reputation score, and profile status.

### Authentication and security information

The implemented service may process:

- Login timestamps.
- Session identifiers.
- Device fingerprint inputs.
- User-agent hashes and device hashes.
- Session trust flags, revocation state, expiry data, and last-seen activity.
- Failed-login and lockout state.
- Multi-factor enrollment and factor status.
- OAuth and phone-OTP authentication events where enabled.

### Reward, task, and gamification information

The implemented service may process:

- Task preferences and onboarding profile data.
- Social profile payloads submitted for task onboarding or compliance checks.
- Task sessions, submissions, verification records, review state, and fraud signals.
- Reward-video session activity, including heartbeat counts, watch time, focus loss, hidden-tab events, and seek violations.
- XP, streaks, quests, achievements, leaderboard state, and daily-task progress.
- Promotional spin attempts, prize outcomes, reward-vault reservations, and unlock requirements.

### Financial and payout information

The implemented service may process:

- Wallet balances and wallet-account breakdowns.
- Reward-ledger entries.
- Wallet transfers and transaction history.
- Withdrawal requests, payout methods, destination labels, destination values, scheduled dates, fees, net amounts, exchange-rate usage, and review state.
- Membership-fee invoices and fee-compliance status.
- Receipt confirmations and non-receipt reports.

### Communications and preference information

The implemented service may process:

- Notification history.
- Communication-channel preferences.
- Delivery queue metadata.
- Support or operational messages sent through in-app, email, push, SMS, or other enabled channels.

### Business, merchant, and campaign information

Where you use advertiser, campaign-manager, merchant, or related features, the service may process campaign content, budgets, reporting metrics, merchant qualification data, order records, escrow events, dispute records, and payout-assignment information.

## 3. How we use information

We use information to:

- Create and manage accounts.
- Authenticate users and protect sessions.
- Verify email, reset passwords, and support MFA.
- Deliver public and authenticated service features.
- Track task completion, evaluate reward eligibility, and prevent fraud.
- Operate gamification, promotions, reward-vault reservations, and membership flows.
- Process wallet activity, transfers, fee checks, and withdrawal workflows.
- Send operational, transactional, promotional, and compliance-related communications.
- Support campaign performance, moderation, analytics, trust and safety, compliance, and dispute handling.
- Maintain logs, audits, debugging, and service reliability.
- Comply with legal, regulatory, and platform-governance obligations.

## 4. Legal and operational bases

Depending on your jurisdiction, we may process information where necessary to:

- Perform our contract with you.
- Operate the service you request.
- Protect the service, users, and platform integrity.
- Comply with legal obligations.
- Pursue legitimate operational, fraud-prevention, security, reporting, or product-improvement interests.
- Send communications where consent or preference settings apply.

## 5. How information may be shared

We may share information with:

- Hosting, database, authentication, storage, and infrastructure providers.
- Messaging and notification providers.
- Payment, merchant, or payout-support providers.
- Compliance, fraud, moderation, analytics, security, or support service providers.
- Internal administrators, reviewers, or authorized operators who need access to run the service.
- Authorities or counterparties where required by law, investigation, compliance review, dispute handling, or protection of rights.

We do not describe every third-party processor in this draft. Production use should add the specific vendor inventory and jurisdictional notices your deployment requires.

## 6. Communications

The platform may send messages through enabled channels such as in-app, email, push, SMS, WhatsApp, and Telegram. Some messages are necessary for account security, verification, password reset, reward status, compliance review, and withdrawal updates.

Delivery may depend on third-party infrastructure and device permissions. Some dispatch flows are implemented on a best-effort basis.

## 7. Cookies, local storage, and device state

The implemented platform uses browser and client-side state for session continuity, security, and product behavior, including session IDs and promotional-spin guest or display state. Production deployment may also use cookies or storage mechanisms provided by integrated authentication services.

If you publish this policy publicly, you should supplement it with your final cookie and consent disclosures for the deployed environment.

## 8. Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to:

- Provide the service.
- Maintain account, wallet, reward, compliance, and audit records.
- Prevent abuse and investigate incidents.
- Comply with legal, financial, tax, regulatory, and recordkeeping obligations.
- Resolve disputes and enforce agreements.

Retention periods may vary by data type, role, transaction history, compliance state, and legal requirements.

## 9. Security

The implemented platform includes security features such as role-based access controls, protected routes, session registration, session revocation, MFA support, lockout-related RPC checks, and audit-related records. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

You are responsible for keeping your credentials, devices, and recovery channels secure.

## 10. Your choices and rights

Depending on your jurisdiction and account role, you may be able to:

- Access or update profile information.
- Change notification preferences where those controls are available.
- Revoke active sessions.
- Reset your password.
- Request support for account or data questions.
- Exercise legal privacy rights such as access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or portability where applicable.

Some records may be retained despite a deletion request where required for security, fraud prevention, compliance, accounting, or legal obligations.

## 11. International processing

Your information may be processed in jurisdictions where service providers, infrastructure, reviewers, or administrators operate. Production publication should include the specific transfer and regional compliance language required for your deployment.

## 12. Children's privacy

This operational draft does not establish a child-directed service. If the final product will be made available to minors or regulated age groups, you should add the required age and parental-consent language before publication.

## 13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the service, vendors, data flows, or legal requirements change. Continued use after changes become effective may be treated as acceptance where permitted by law.
