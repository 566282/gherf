# Help Center

Operational help center draft based on implemented platform behavior as of 2026-08-05.

## 1. How to get support

For support requests, contact the help desk at helpdesk@go4wealth.org

When contacting support, include:

- Your account email.
- The page or route where the issue occurred.
- Approximate date and time of the issue.
- Any status message or error text shown.
- Relevant transaction, withdrawal, task, or reservation ID when available.

## 2. Quick start checklist

If you are new to the platform:

1. Create your account from the signup page.
2. Verify your email.
3. Sign in and complete task onboarding.
4. Review your profile and membership tier.
5. Open tasks and gamification pages for daily progression.
6. Track reward and wallet activity before requesting withdrawals.

## 3. Account access and login help

Supported access paths include:

- Email and password.
- OAuth providers.
- Phone OTP for existing users where enabled.
- Password reset through forgot-password.

If you cannot sign in:

1. Confirm your email and password are correct.
2. Use password reset if needed.
3. Wait and retry if lock protection was triggered by failed attempts.
4. Review active sessions and revoke unfamiliar sessions after access is restored.
5. Contact helpdesk@go4wealth.org if the issue persists.

## 4. Profile and onboarding support

To avoid task and verification issues:

1. Complete your profile details.
2. Save preferred task types in onboarding.
3. Add required social profile payload data used for compliance-aware checks.
4. Keep your account details accurate and up to date.

## 5. Daily tasks and reward claim troubleshooting

For verified task sessions:

1. Start tasks from the task page.
2. Keep the session active until completion thresholds are met.
3. Avoid hidden tabs, repeated focus loss, and seek violations.
4. Wait for claim availability before submitting a claim.

If a reward is not credited:

1. Check task status and notification history.
2. Confirm the session met watch and verification requirements.
3. Check for review-required or anti-abuse flags.
4. Contact helpdesk@go4wealth.org with your task/session details.

## 6. Wallet, transfer, and withdrawal support

Before requesting a withdrawal:

1. Confirm membership tier eligibility.
2. Check min and max withdrawal limits.
3. Review supported payout methods.
4. Confirm destination details and scheduled date.

Important policy reminders:

- Free-tier members cannot withdraw funds.
- Processing fees may apply.
- Requests may be held by policy, plan, or compliance workflows.
- Outstanding fee obligations may block or delay eligibility.

If a payout is delayed:

1. Review request status and wallet notifications.
2. Confirm whether compliance review is in progress.
3. Use receipt confirmation or non-receipt reporting flows when prompted.
4. Contact helpdesk@go4wealth.org with the withdrawal request ID.

## 7. Promotional spin and reward-vault support

The promotional wheel may create reservations instead of immediate payouts.

If a promotional reward is not released, verify:

1. Registration completion.
2. Verification completion.
3. Referral and membership requirements.
4. Reservation expiration status.

Contact helpdesk@go4wealth.org with reservation identifiers if release conditions appear complete but status does not update.

## 8. Notification and communication support

The platform supports in-app and configured external channels for operational updates.

If you miss an expected message:

1. Check in-app notification history first.
2. Verify your account email and preferences.
3. Check whether the update was informational only in dashboard status.
4. Contact helpdesk@go4wealth.org for verification on critical events.

## 9. Suspensions, reviews, and appeals

Some account or transaction states may be restricted due to security, compliance, or abuse controls.

If your account or request is under review:

1. Check the latest status message and notice details.
2. Follow any in-app guidance provided.
3. Submit requested documentation if applicable.
4. Contact helpdesk@go4wealth.org for escalation status.
