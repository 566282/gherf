# Contact Go4Wealth

Operational contact-page draft based on implemented platform behavior as of 2026-08-05.

## 1. How to reach us

### Support channels

- Telegram: @helpdesk_go4wealth

### Business inquiries email

- helpdesk@go4wealth.org

## 2. Office hours

Open Monday through Sunday 8am to 10pm.

## 3. What to include when contacting us

To help the team resolve requests quickly, include:

- Your account email.
- The route or page where the issue happened.
- Date and time of the event.
- Any on-screen status or error message.
- Relevant IDs (task session, withdrawal request, transaction, reservation, or notification) when available.

## 4. Contact scenarios

### Account access and security

Contact us for login issues, password reset difficulties, session concerns, or account verification support.

### Task and reward workflows

Contact us if tasks remain unverified, claims do not process as expected, or reward status appears inconsistent.

### Wallet and withdrawal support

Contact us for payout delays, withdrawal status clarification, receipt confirmation concerns, and compliance-review questions.

### Promotional reward support

Contact us if reward-vault reservations appear blocked after all required conditions have been completed.

## 5. Trust and operations

Go4Wealth is designed as a trust-first platform with role-based controls, verification-aware workflows, and operational visibility across user, reward, and payout flows.

For high-priority operational issues, contact helpdesk@go4wealth.org and include full incident context for escalation.
