# Go4Wealth Advertiser Pages

Operational advertiser-page draft based on implemented platform behavior as of 2026-08-05.

## Purpose

This page set is designed for advertiser operators, campaign managers, and enterprise decision teams evaluating whether Go4Wealth provides enough governance, clarity, and measurable control for campaign execution.

## Core narrative

Go4Wealth advertiser experiences are built around five pillars:

1. Verification-aware onboarding
2. Reserve-based budget discipline
3. Campaign lifecycle control
4. Structured review and anti-abuse posture
5. Reporting visibility and export-ready analytics

## Content blocks

### 1) Business onboarding and verification workflow

Advertisers can register a business entity, provide website and contact details, and progress through verification states before unlocking full operational capability. This protects campaign quality and creates a consistent trust baseline before budget activation.

### 2) Funding reserve and budget governance

The advertiser console exposes reserve balance, committed spend, and available budget in one view. Teams can fund operations, monitor spend pressure, and avoid over-allocation while keeping budget decisions auditable.

### 3) Campaign lifecycle controls from one workspace

Advertiser and campaign-manager roles access a role-protected business route where they can create campaigns, edit active plans, and govern lifecycle transitions without switching contexts.

### 4) Submission review and verification posture

Campaign outcomes are supported by review-first verification pathways. Evidence requirements and verification methods are designed to reduce abuse risk while preserving legitimate participant conversion flow.

### 5) Cross-functional modules for growth operations

The business workspace includes campaign management plus dedicated operations for communications, analytics reporting, and gamification administration, enabling coordinated execution across teams.

### 6) KPI visibility for decision velocity

Advertiser analytics surfaces performance signals such as CTR, conversions, ROI, campaign status distribution, and engagement patterns that support faster optimization and clearer stakeholder updates.

### 7) Export-ready reports for finance and compliance

Report exports in CSV and JSON formats support downstream workflows for governance, audits, and executive reporting without requiring manual data reshaping.

### 8) CMS-governed public advertiser messaging

Public advertiser pages are CMS-driven, allowing administrators to refine onboarding copy, policy messaging, proof points, and calls to action without a deployment cycle.

## Suggested CTA framework

- Primary CTA: Start advertiser onboarding
- Secondary CTA: Open campaign management
- Tertiary CTA: View analytics and reporting

## Suggested trust markers

- Role-based business workspace access
- Verification-gated activation flow
- Budget and reserve transparency
- Exportable operational reporting
- CMS-governed page revision control
